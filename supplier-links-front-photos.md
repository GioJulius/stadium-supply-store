## How the links were found

Twenty-eight of the listings carry a `supplier-<album id>` tag from the import,
so the id came straight off the product. The other five — Bellingham 5, Mbeumo
19, both Aston Villas and the PSG fourth — came off Instagram or a saved album
folder and have no tag; those were matched by searching the supplier catalogue
by title. Every one of the 33 ids was then confirmed against the supplier's own
album listing, which is where the album titles above come from.

**Reading Yupoo needs a real browser.** `curl` and `urllib` get a 404 without
`?uid=1`, and with it they get a JS shell with no title in it — the album titles
only exist after the page renders. The listing endpoint
`/albums?tab=gallery&page=N` does return parseable HTML, and paging it to
exhaustion gives all 4 800 albums with their ids and titles in one pass; that is
the cheap way to resolve or search ids in bulk.

## What "front-facing" means here

A photograph of the whole garment from the front. Twenty-one of the 33 have only
back views and close-ups. Three distinct shapes of problem:

- **Back view only.** The supplier shoots the back first for anything with a
  print on it. Bellingham 5, Mbeumo 19, both Home Shirts, the Barcelona home
  fan and player, the three long-sleeve player versions, the France training
  set, and the France, England, Liverpool and Real Madrid half-zips.
- **Close-ups only.** Both Chinese Knot pairs, the Barcelona away kids kit and
  the AC Milan away player. No whole garment at any angle.
- **Front exists but is not the lead.** The 12 marked ✓ — those need no new
  photograph at all, just a reorder.
