# Final Grouped-Media Duplicate Audit

## Scope and evidence

This audit covers the **98 supplied product-media groups** listed in `kit-consolidation-register.md`. Each source file remains recorded in that register beside its assigned kit identity, price tier, and retained product decision. On 2026-08-27, the active Shopify catalog was retrieved across three pages, returning **102 active products**; all reviewed records used for customer discovery expose four S–XL variants.

The audit treats visual similarity as insufficient for merging. A group was merged only when the club, season, design, personalisation, and version aligned. Otherwise, it remains a distinct customer-facing product even when club and season overlap. Every source still image in each reviewed multi-image group was attached to its retained product gallery. The owner directed that the three supplied product MP4s be deferred, so they are not counted as missing still-image evidence.

## Outcome

The catalog contains named, non-generic customer-facing product records with complete supplied **still-image** galleries, reviewed Fan/Player/Retro pricing, and S–XL variants. The audit does not fabricate ratings, reviews, or other user-generated content. The three owner-deferred product MP4 files remain documented in `shopify-gallery-audit-latest.md` and are excluded from further action.
