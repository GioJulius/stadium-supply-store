across three zips, seventeen folders and fifty-four loose files — against the

Web's history sync stops at 9:14 PM and Portugal was sent from 9:10. Where the
client sent two of the same kit the pair resolves itself (one fan, one player).

## Worth fixing while we are in here

Downloading the live Netherlands images to compare against turned up something
separate: **eight of the seventeen Netherlands listings have a photograph of a
care label, a hangtag or a swatch of fabric as their lead image** — `2026 World
Cup Netherlands Away Fan Version` leads on a "MOVE TO ZERO" tag, `Home Kids Kit`
on a Nike "BREATHE" tag, `2024 UEFA Home Fan Version` on a close-up of orange
cloth. Those are the listings a shopper sees in the grid.

The 22 photographs above that duplicate an existing listing are the fix. Rather
than discard them as duplicates or list them twice, append them to the listings
they match — which also answers the client's standing complaint that listings
carry a single photograph.
