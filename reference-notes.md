# Reference Notes

The visual direction calls for a high-contrast editorial commerce experience: oversized condensed display type, a restrained black-and-white palette, deliberate horizontal rules, section numbering, and immersive collection moments rather than a conventional catalog-first landing page.

Stadium Supply’s public Instagram profile describes the brand as “From the stands to the streets” across football, rugby, NBA, and F1. The storefront will focus on football kits while carrying this sport-culture perspective through its language and navigation.

The supplied Stadium Supply photograph, showing two football shirts against a blue sky with the Stadium Supply wordmark, will anchor the hero section. The tall reference image is treated as a layout and editorial-motion reference rather than an in-store product image.

A recent Stadium Supply post features a Manchester United Away 26/27 football shirt. Its public image will be used for one initial, clearly editable Shopify preview product while the full merchant catalog is transferred through Shopify Admin.

The public Instagram information highlight did not expose a readable product price in the accessible viewer. The initial product will therefore use an explicitly provisional Shopify price that the merchant can replace in Shopify Admin before launch.

The upper editorial reference confirms a white-to-silver split composition with a visibly torn vertical seam, a compact utility navigation at the top edges, an oversized condensed wordmark, and a single sharp accent colour. These cues will be translated into the Stadium Supply layout without copying the source brand or fashion imagery.

The middle reference sections preserve the full-height central seam while allowing concise large-format messaging to sit on a low-contrast image field. Stadium Supply will adapt this into a restrained blue, bone, and ink palette with captions that describe football culture rather than the reference campaign.

The closing reference confirms a direct, bottom-anchored drop call to action. The Stadium Supply hero will use this same confidence through an oversized “New arrivals” treatment and an action that leads to the shoppable catalog.

The public Stadium Supply information highlight confirms that orders are processed weekly, payment is required in full before processing, delivery is estimated at 10–15 business days, and refunds or exchanges are not guaranteed due to the nature of the products. Product detail copy should keep these policy points available without presenting unverified shipping guarantees.

The public price-list highlight specifies South African Rand prices: fan-version football jerseys are **R450**, player-version jerseys are **R650**, retro jerseys are **R700**, and kiddies sets are **R400**. Add-ons for sleeves, patches, or printing are each **R50**. Product cards must use these verified category price points rather than invented prices.

After the expanded Shopify catalog resolves, the storefront renders the first 24 available pieces in its initial grid while preserving category filters and sorting controls. This confirms the catalog has a deliberate paginated first-page presentation even as the active Shopify product count grows.
