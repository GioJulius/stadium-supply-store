## Duplicate-risk findings

The catalog-wide review covered all 72 active products in two Shopify pages. The audit found **unique product titles and handles** across the current set. The named and category-defined baseline consists of the Manchester United Away kit, Mbeumo 19 Black Kit, and Drops 01–06. Drops 07–70 are distinct, sequential generic records tagged `Editable Drop`, each with one distinct lead-media URL returned by Shopify. No duplicate title, handle, or lead-media URL was identified in the review.

> The main catalog risk is **not technical duplication**; it is merchandising ambiguity. Generic drops must remain editable until their media is matched to a confirmed kit identity and price category.

## Controlled next steps

The next step is not to create more generic products. Instead, the supplied images will be grouped into identifiable kit records in a mapping register. Each mapped group will identify its Shopify product, price tier, media file, title, and size availability. Only after that register is reviewed will existing generic records be renamed or new items created.
