## Where the labels come from

Two sources, and they agree:

1. **The photographs**, read by eye against the crests, sponsors, manufacturer
   marks and kit designs.
2. **The client's own captions in the WhatsApp thread.** Every photo message
   carries a one-word garment type — `retro`, `retro long sleeve`, `fan
   version`, `fan version long sleeve`, `player version`, `player version long
   sleeve`, `kids set`, `training set`, `half zip`, `full zip`, `jacket`,
   `windbreaker`, `hoodie` — and each team's block is bracketed by a plain text
   message naming the club.

I pulled 101 of the roughly 112 caption messages off WhatsApp Web. **The window
from 9:10:15 to 9:13:49 PM would not load** — WhatsApp's history sync stops
there and neither scrolling nor the reload control brings it back. That window
is the Portugal opener (#1–#31), so those garment types are read from the
photographs alone rather than confirmed against a caption. Everything from
9:14 PM on is caption-confirmed.

Note that several messages are **albums**, not single photos — one caption over
2 to 6 images. That is why 149 photographs carry only ~112 captions, and it is
also the answer to the client's standing complaint: these arrive already grouped
into multi-view listings.
