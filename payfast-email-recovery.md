# Payfast Incorrect Verification Email Recovery

## Current issue

The Payfast merchant signup was created with the incorrect, non-existent verification address `stadiumcustomers@gmail.com`. The intended address is `stadiumscustomers@gmail.com`. Do not create, access, or rely on the mistyped address.

## Recommended recovery route

1. Contact Payfast Merchant Support and state that the merchant account was created with a typographical error in the verification email before activation.
2. Ask support to change the primary email from `stadiumcustomers@gmail.com` to `stadiumscustomers@gmail.com` and resend the account-verification email.
3. Give only the information Payfast requires to identify the application, such as the registered business or applicant name, registration reference, and the intended email. The client should provide identity, bank, tax, and other verification documents directly to Payfast.
4. Once the corrected message arrives, the client should complete verification from the intended mailbox. The Payfast account should remain owned by the client/business that receives payouts.

## Official support routes

Payfast’s Account Management knowledge base includes an article titled **“How do I change my primary email?”** and its support portal offers ticket submission. Payfast directs merchants to its Help Centre, live chat, and `support@payfast.help` for account questions. Its published contact centre number is **+27 21 300 4455**, with listed operating hours of Monday–Friday 08:00–18:00 GMT+2 and Saturday 08:00–14:00 GMT+2.
